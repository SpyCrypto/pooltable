Why PoolTable

PoolTable is community infrastructure for Cardano. Our mission — from the playbook — is to strengthen Cardano by building community; our vision is to become a trusted community hub. This pool is built around Observe → Build → Connect: we learn the network, build useful infrastructure, and connect people to it.

We run an independent, learning-driven operation — US-based, hands-on, and transparent. We don’t promise the biggest rewards or the largest pool. We build credibility through consistent operation, honest communication, and real participation in the Cardano ecosystem.

OBSERVE

We stay current with Cardano — protocol upgrades, Leios, and the wider ecosystem — before adopting anything.

BUILD

Independent, reliable infrastructure. We reinvest into resilience, monitoring, and documentation — not shortcuts.

CONNECT

We share what we learn, engage with the community, and support informed delegation.

Delegate to PoolTable

  1. Get a wallet — Eternl, Yoroi or Typhon store your ADA and let you stake.
  2. Select PoolTable (PTBL) — search the pool by ticker PTBL in your wallet, or paste the pool ID (shown here the moment we’re live on-chain).
  3. Confirm & activate — your stake shows as delegating within 1–2 epochs; rewards appear after ~2 full epochs. No lock-in: undelegate or switch anytime.
